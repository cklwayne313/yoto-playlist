How to use it

Animal names and animal sounds are the cheapest language wins there are: one word, one sound, and the child gets an immediate reaction. This card runs from naming (I Have A Pet, What Do You Hear?) to the sound itself (Old MacDonald, BINGO). Sing the sound and wait — don't fill it in for him.

Notes

  • Deliberate overlap: Old MacDonald and BINGO also appear on farm-songs. They are the two best sound-imitation songs in the library, so they're repeated here for a different job.
  • New to the library: Zoo, I Have A Pet, What Do You Hear, Three Little Kittens, Five Little Speckled Frogs, Little Bo Peep, Hey Diddle Diddle.
  • What Do You Hear? is the one to use face-to-face — pause after the sound and let him copy it, not just listen.
  • Hey Diddle Diddle is only 1:10 and is pure rhythm; use it as the warm-up.
