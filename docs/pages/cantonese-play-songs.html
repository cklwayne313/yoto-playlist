Trimming the intro

Most 志安哥哥 videos open with the same ~10.5 s branded jingle (聽歌學講廣東話) followed by a gap of varying length, so the cut point is measured per track:

  • Trim is where the song itself starts — cut everything before it.
  • — means there is nothing worth cutting.
  • 0:10 to 0:15 is the full jingle.

Measured by matching the jingle's envelope against a reference copy, then finding the first silence gap after it.
