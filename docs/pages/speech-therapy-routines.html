How to use it

The same six routines every day, in song. Routines are where language sticks, because the words repeat at the same time and place: shoes, teeth, hands, bath, tidy up, bed. Sing the line before you do the step, then wait.

Notes

  • Deliberate overlap: shoes, bath, clean-up, hello and goodbye also sit on super-simple-favourites. This card is the routine sequence, so they belong here.
  • The Bath Song and The Hand Washing Song both use body-part + verb frames — the most useful pattern in the set.
  • Pick one routine a week and use only that track. Six routines at once is noise.
