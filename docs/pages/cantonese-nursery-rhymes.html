Why these songs (and not just any 童謠)

Speech and language therapists consistently recommend the same handful of things, and these 12 songs are chosen to hit all of them:

Technique What it does Songs here
Short + repetitive more chances to hear the same word 點蟲蟲 (0:39), 氹氹轉 (0:35), 三隻白白豬
Action / finger play movement anchors the word in memory 點蟲蟲, 洗白白, 氹氹轉
Onomatopoeia easiest sounds to copy first 有隻雀仔跌落水 (唧唧), 五隻鴨仔 (嘎嘎)
Fill-in-the-gap pause and let him finish the line 打開蚊帳, 太陽伯伯
Daily routine same words, same time, every day 洗白白 (bathtime), 太陽伯伯 (morning)
Vocabulary sets words that group together 紅橙黃綠藍靛紫 (colours), 三隻白白豬 + 五隻鴨仔 (counting), 大笨象 (verbs)
Tone practice Cantonese is 9 tones; melody trains pitch 月光光, 世界真細小

Two of the songs are deliberately very short (0:35, 0:39) so you can play them five times in a row without it feeling like a chore — repetition is the point.

Order: easiest first

Sorted for a 2-year-old, not by theme. Three things make a song "easy" at that age, and the top of the list scores high on all three:

Tier What it means Tracks
1 — join in straight away under 1:40, 1–2 short lines, big hand/body actions, he can do it before he can say it 1–6
2 — knows the pattern 2:20+, one repeated structure (counting down, same question), more words per line 7–10
3 — listening, not singing abstract or literary lyrics, slower melody; good for calm-down and tone, not for joining in yet 11–12

So the card opens with 點蟲蟲 (39 seconds, two phrases, finger play) and closes with 月光光 (a lullaby). If he only ever hears the first five, that's fine — that is the part built for him right now.

How to actually use it

The recording is only half of it — the interaction is what moves speech along.

  1. Pick 2–3 songs for the week, not all 12. Rotate next week.
  2. Do the actions with him. Hand on his hand if he'll let you.
  3. Pause before the last word and wait 5 seconds. Even a look or a gesture counts as a turn.
  4. Slow down and get face-to-face so he sees your mouth.
  5. Sing it yourself, no music, after he knows it — same words, same order. Then say it as a rhyme (誦) rather than singing; spoken prosody is closer to speech.
  6. Don't correct pronunciation; just say the word back correctly.

On the speech delay

Songs are a strong daily support, but they aren't an assessment. If you're worried:

  • In Hong Kong: start at the 母嬰健康院 (Maternal and Child Health Centre); they can refer to the 兒童體能智力測驗服務 (Child Assessment Service) and speech therapy. Private 言語治療師 waiting lists are usually shorter.
  • Elsewhere: GP / health visitor → speech-language pathologist. Early referral is the single biggest factor, and "he might just be late" is worth ruling out rather than waiting on.
  • Keep Cantonese as the home language. Bilingual children sometimes have a smaller vocabulary in each language while the total is normal — dropping Cantonese doesn't help; rich Cantonese input does.

I'm not a clinician — this is the standard guidance from Speech and Language UK, the NHS and Leeds Community Healthcare, applied to Cantonese material.

About ads

You said you want to avoid ad-heavy videos, so here's the honest situation:

  • On the Yoto card: zero ads. It plays local audio files, no network involved.
  • On YouTube: none of these can have mid-roll ads. Mid-rolls only exist on videos of roughly 8+ minutes; every track here is under 3 minutes, so the worst case is one skippable pre-roll at the start.
  • What I avoided when choosing: compilation videos (the 12-minute and 45-minute 串燒 uploads), piano-only/伴奏 tracks, and reaction/re-upload channels — those are where the long unskippable ad breaks live.
  • What I can't control: whether a clip gets a pre-roll. That depends on the channel's monetisation and your account, and it isn't visible from the link. YouTube Premium removes it; the Yoto card never shows it either way.
  • HK - Topic uploads (the clean label audio) mostly don't exist for these songs — the ones that do are mainland labels (会声花儿组合, 小蓓蕾), which would break the Hong Kong-only rule. So the HK creators' own channels are the cleanest option available.

Notes

  • #9 打開蚊帳 has embedding disabled on YouTube (the link works fine in a browser/app, it just won't play inside another site).
