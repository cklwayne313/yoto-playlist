Notes

  • The card is 30 tracks, not 15. Each song appears twice: once sung, once as a "- Sing-a-long" repeat. Those repeats are the same recordings with the vocals pulled back, so this playlist keeps the 15 sung versions only — 33 minutes instead of 68. Say the word if you want the sing-alongs added as a second pass.
  • Artists are mixed, because no single channel has all 15:
  • Super Simple Songs — 1, 3, 6, 7, 9, 10, 11, 12, 13, 14
  • Cocomelon — 2, 4
  • HooplaKidz — 8
  • The Rainbow Collections — 5
  • Little Baby Bum — 15
  • #15 is sometimes titled "Hop Little Bunnies"; the Little Baby Bum version (1:50) is the closest match to the card's 1:51.
  • #5 Teddy Bears' Picnic is a 1907 song, so every version is an arrangement. The card's is Yoto's; this one is a straight kids' rendition.
