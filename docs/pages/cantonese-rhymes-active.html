Why two cards, not one

The seller's card holds 30 songs and 1h18m. That is a catalogue: a two-year-old cannot choose from it, and a card that long never repeats the same five songs, which is the part that builds words. Splitting it by energy gives two cards that each do one job — one to jump and sing to, one to wind down to.

Two differences from the seller's list, both honest:

  • their 貼心歌 (1:53) is the channel's 點心歌 (2:04) — dim sum song; no upload is called 貼心歌.
  • their 何家公雞何家猜 is the channel's 何家小雞何家猜 (2:45).
  • 五隻鴨仔 is not here: it already lives on cantonese-nursery-rhymes.

How to use it

Play the first five on repeat; they are short enough that five in a row is still under ten minutes. Sing the line, then stop and look at him — 有隻八腳蜘蛛 and 老伯塊田係牧場 both have a pause built in for exactly that.
