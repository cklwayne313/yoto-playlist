Why two cards, not one

The seller's card holds 30 songs and 1h18m. That is a catalogue: a two-year-old cannot choose from it, and a card that long never repeats the same five songs, which is the part that builds words. Splitting it by energy gives two cards that each do one job — one to dance to, one to listen to.

Two differences from the seller's list, both honest:

  • their 貼心歌 (1:53) is the channel's 點心歌 (2:04) — dim sum song; no upload is called 貼心歌.
  • their 何家公雞何家猜 is the channel's 何家小雞何家猜 (2:45).
  • 五隻鴨仔 is not here: it already lives on cantonese-nursery-rhymes.

How to use it

Use this at the end of the day, or when he is already calm. Nothing here is built for joining in — 詠鵝 and 小時候 are for tone and vocabulary, not singing along. Start with 晚安曲 and let it run.
