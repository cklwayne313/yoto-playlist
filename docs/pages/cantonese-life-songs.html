Trimming the intro

Most 志安哥哥 videos open with the same ~10.5 s branded jingle (聽歌學講廣東話) followed by a gap. The gap varies, so the trim point is per track, measured, not assumed:

  • Trim column below is where the song itself starts. Cut everything before it.
  • 0:01 means there is almost nothing to cut — just the lead-in.
  • 齊齊拍 is the one here with the full jingle: cut 0:12.
  • Long lead-ins worth cutting: 吹泡泡 0:05, 小腳板 0:04, 口罩超人 0:03.

Measured by matching the jingle's envelope against a reference copy, then finding the first silence gap after it — not by ear and not by guess.
