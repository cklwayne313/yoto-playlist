Notes

  • The card is 30 tracks: 15 songs + 15 instrumentals. This playlist keeps the 15 sung versions.
  • Sequence songs (The Ants Go Marching, Old Lady Who Swallowed a Fly) are the most useful here: predictable order, one new word per verse.
  • There's a Hole in My Bucket is a call-and-response — split the two parts between you.
