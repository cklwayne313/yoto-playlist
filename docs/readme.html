Covers

Playlists that mirror a real Yoto card keep that card's artwork as cover.jpg, pulled from the product page. Upload it as the playlist image when you create the Make Your Own card, and it looks the same on the player.

  • It's the publisher's artwork, so it's for your own card — don't redistribute it.
  • cantonese-nursery-rhymes uses Yoto's Nursery Rhymes art as a thematic stand-in; no Cantonese card exists.
  • speech-therapy-english uses Ms. Rachel's I'm So Happy art — the closest Yoto card to the theme, since her show began as a speech-and-language resource.
  • speech-therapy-cantonese uses Yoto's Animal Sounds art; animal sounds are the first thing a late talker imitates.
  • The eight target cards use eight different Super Simple characters, one per card, so no two covers repeat: the Roundabouts pig (Animal Songs), the yellow Noodle & Pals friend (Action Songs), a Bumble Num (Food Songs), Carl's Rescue Crew (Routine Songs), a Rhymington Square monster (Social Songs), Lulu the Owl (Feeling Songs), the ABC Hop rabbit (動物歌) and a Roundabouts penguin (日常歌). Each sits in its own painted scene — meadow, sunburst, kitchen, morning sky, party wall, sand rings, garden, night sky. The characters are Super Simple's; the backgrounds and lettering are ours.
  • super-simple-favourites has a custom cover: Super Simple's Noodle character on flat cream, titled My Favourites.
  • cantonese-rhymes-active (跳跳歌) and cantonese-rhymes-calm (慢慢歌) are a matched pair from the same Hong Kong channel: one daytime meadow with coral lettering, one dusk meadow with indigo. No character on either — a collection card does not need one.
  • Every custom cover carries the small hand-lettered HimHim mark, centred above the title in the same two colours every time — ink + coral, cream + coral on the dark card. It's the family's label, never a publisher logo, so the nine custom cards read as one set on the shelf without pretending to be official cards.

The cover system

Custom covers are built from two stored assets per card, so a re-typeset never depends on temp files:

.opencode/skills/yoto-cover/assets/<card>-plate.jpg painted background, no character .opencode/skills/yoto-cover/assets/<card>-character.png official art, background removed

scripts/build_cover.py lays them out, scripts/cutout.py keys a character off chroma green, and one command rebuilds all nine:

bash bash .opencode/skills/yoto-cover/scripts/rebuild_custom_covers.sh python3 .opencode/print/build_print.py && python3 .opencode/print/build_vendor.py

Layout rules: 7% side margins; the mark in its own band at the top; the title centred under it, two lines, SONGS on the second line; the block sized to the space above the character, never touching it.

Adding a playlist

Send a YouTube link — a playlist, a single video, or a card you've seen. I'll add playlists/<name>/reference.md with the tracks pulled out, in order, each with its link, and notes on anything swapped or missing.

Printing the covers

Every card has one image: playlists/<name>/cover.jpg at 638 × 1011 px, 300 DPI — 54 × 85.6 mm, the size of a Yoto card. That same file is what the app accepts and what the print sheets embed. print/ holds A4 / US Letter sheets with nine labels each and crop marks. Print at 100% / Actual size — never "fit to page". Rebuild with:

bash python3 .opencode/print/build_print.py

See print/README.md for paper and trimming notes.

Sending covers to a printer

vendor/trim/ has every card at 54 × 85.5 mm, 300 DPI, no bleed; vendor/bleed/ has the same art with 3 mm bleed. Send whichever your printer asks for — trim art into a bleed workflow gives white edges. See vendor/README.md.

Browsing it as a website

docs/ is a generated static site: the ranked shelf, one page per playlist with clickable links and covers, plus the ranking and this readme. Rebuild after editing any markdown:

bash python3 .opencode/site/build.py

To publish on GitHub Pages: push the repo, then Settings → Pages → Deploy from a branch → main / /docs. The site works from a subpath, so https://<user>.github.io/<repo>/ is fine.

Using a playlist

  1. Open its reference.md and listen through the links.
  2. Put the audio on a Make Your Own playlist at yotoplay.com (Make Your Own → create playlist).
  3. Link the blank card: Yoto app → Players → your player → Link a card, then hold the card flat on the player's NFC spot until it beeps.
