Playlist ranking

ShelfRankingRead me

A single place to check before you put a card on the shelf. Ranked for one listener: a 2-year-old boy with a possible speech delay, Cantonese at home, English cards.

Order = speech value first, then age fit. Fidelity only says how close the links are to a real card; it does not change what he gets out of it.

Last checked: 25 Nov 2026.

How to read this

Column What it measures
Speech Does it help talking? Repetition, short lines, actions, onomatopoeia, slow clear vocals. Not "is it nice music".
Age 2 Is the content right for a 2-year-old — pace, length, concepts.
Fidelity How closely the YouTube links match the actual card. ★★★★★ = same recordings, durations within seconds. ★★ = right songs, different arrangements. — = no card exists.

Verdicts

  • CORE — daily rotation, the ones doing the work
  • GOOD — solid, rotate in
  • FUN — entertainment, not speech practice
  • LATER — good card, wrong age for now

Re-checking later

Links rot and channels rename. To re-audit every link with real metadata:

bash YT=/home/wayne/Documents/youtube/.venv/bin/yt-dlp grep -rhoE 'https://youtu\.be/[A-Za-z0-9_-]+' playlists/*/reference.md | sort -u \ | sed 's#https://youtu.be/#https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=#' \ | xargs $YT --skip-download --print '%(id)s | %(duration_string)s | %(uploader)s | %(title)s'

Anything that errors is dead. To catch a wrong song rather than a dead one, compare the printed title and duration against the row in reference.md.
