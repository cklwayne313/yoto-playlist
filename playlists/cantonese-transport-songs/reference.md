# 交通工具 — Miss Ka Foo transport songs

**Inspired by:** the 嘉芙姐姐 transport playlist in the screenshots you sent
(帶你遊香港, 坐巴士, 搭的士 …). Every track is her own upload from
[嘉芙姐姐 Miss Ka Foo](https://www.youtube.com/channel/UCZgs3pLaFisZ-TKVOwFEe8Q).
**Cover:** `cover.jpg` — a painted scene, titled **交通工具**. No character: these are collection cards, and a borrowed mascot on a Cantonese song card reads as a sticker. Background and lettering are ours. Not a Yoto card.
**Tracks:** 16 · **Runtime:** 34:13 · **Status:** verified

## Why this one first

Vehicles are the highest-interest vocabulary a two-year-old has: he will point at
a bus before he will say "apple". Every song here names one thing, makes its
sound, and repeats — 消防車, 救護車 and 警車 are the onomatopoeia tracks.

## Trimming the intro

Her videos have **no shared jingle** — unlike 志安哥哥's 10.5 s opener — so the trim
is only the lead-in before the music starts:

- **Trim** is where the song begins; nothing here needs more than 0:03 cut.
- `—` means start at 0:00.
