## Why these extra ten

The six you picked are a start; the rest fill in the routines a 2-year-old
actually lives through, which is where the language lands:

- **Body parts and actions** — One Little Finger, Head Shoulders Knees And Toes
- **Opposites and gestures** — Open Shut Them
- **Getting dressed** — Put On Your Shoes
- **Bath and tidying up** — The Bath Song, Clean Up Song
- **Greeting and goodbye** — Hello Hello! (yours), See You Later, Alligator
- **Animals and "I can"** — Yes, I Can!, Five Little Ducks
- **Choosing and answering** — Do You Like Broccoli Ice Cream? (yes/no, and the
  silly food swaps get a laugh every time)

All sixteen are the official Super Simple channel, none are compilations, and
every one is under 3:40.

## Cover build

- **Character art:** Super Simple's own Noodle, from the
  [`Me!` key art](https://supersimple.com/wp-content/uploads/2020/08/songs_me-noodleandpals_thumbnail_website-800x800_en.jpg).
- **Background:** the lime sunburst was replaced with flat cream by the image
  model, with the drawing left untouched. The flattened file is kept here as
  `cover-source.jpg`, so the card can be rebuilt.
- **Composed** with the `yoto-cover` skill:

```bash
python3 .opencode/skills/yoto-cover/scripts/make_cover.py \
  --art playlists/super-simple-favourites/cover-source.jpg \
  --title MY FAVOURITES \
  --out playlists/super-simple-favourites/cover.jpg
```

- Output 1200×1902, cream `#FFFFE8`, blue `#0392D4`, character 1120px wide,
  top of head at y=700.
- Not official Yoto art, and not a Yoto product. Personal card only.
