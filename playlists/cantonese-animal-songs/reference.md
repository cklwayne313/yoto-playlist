# 動物朋友 — Miss Ka Foo animal songs

**Inspired by:** 嘉芙姐姐's dinosaur and animal songs in the screenshots you sent.
The four dinosaurs were too few for a card on their own, and she has only those
four — so this card widens to the animals around them: pandas, a lion, the forest
and the sea.
**Cover:** `cover.jpg` — the purple Rhymington monster (Super Simple's art, used as the series mascot) on a painted scene, titled **動物朋友**. Background and lettering are ours. Not a Yoto card.
**Tracks:** 9 · **Runtime:** 21:15 · **Status:** verified

## What it is

Nine animal songs, each naming one thing and making its sound. The dinosaurs do
the describing work (big, three-horned, long-necked, plated); the panda, lion and
sea songs do the naming and the noises, which is what he can copy first.

## Trimming the intro

No jingle on her uploads; only the lead-in:

- 熊貓好朋友 → **0:07** (the longest on the card)
- 三角龍 → **0:04**
- everything else → **0:02**
