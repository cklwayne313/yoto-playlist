## Notes

- **Shape of the card:** big opener (My Happy Song), a short greeting, then a run
  of movement songs (walk outside, bicycle, spin around, pop the bubbles), and
  it lands gently on "Here You Are, Thank You" — a thank-you song as the closer
  is clearly deliberate.
- **#6 If You're Happy** is the one duration mismatch: 1:51 on the card vs 1:32
  on the video. Same song, slightly different edit/mix. Nothing to fix — just
  don't be surprised the video is shorter.
- **#13** is the "Spin Around" version, not the plain "If You're Happy And You
  Know It". Don't buy/substitute the wrong one.
- 40:59 total; no swaps needed, all 14 have videos.
