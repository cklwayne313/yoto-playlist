# Farm songs

**Inspired by:** Super Simple [Farm Songs](https://us.yotoplay.com/products/super-simple-farm-songs)
**Cover:** `cover.jpg` — the card's own art, 1200×1902, from the Yoto product page
**Tracks:** 15 · **Runtime:** ~40 min · **Status:** track list agreed, audio not added yet

A farm/animal song card built from Super Simple's catalogue. The original card
has 15 tracks; 4 of them are album-only with no video anywhere, so they're
swapped for songs that do have official uploads — every track below can be
auditioned before it goes on the card.
