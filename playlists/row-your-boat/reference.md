## Notes

- The card is 30 tracks: 15 songs + 15 instrumentals of the same songs. This playlist keeps the 15 sung versions.
- Super Simple recordings for most; Cocomelon, Little Baby Bum, HooplaKidz and Mother Goose Club fill the gaps.
- Durations differ from the card because these are different arrangements — the running order and song list are the match.

## Swaps

Closest-length replacements, so each download sits nearer the card's timing:

- `Head, Shoulders, Knees and Toes` → Cocomelon - Nursery Rhymes, 3:03 (was a 1:53 Super Simple "Speeding Up" edit).
- `Humpty Dumpty` → Baby Shark - Pinkfong Kids' Songs & Stories, 2:16 (was a 1:18 Super Simple clip).
- `Itsy Bitsy Spider` → Super Simple Songs - Topic, 1:59 (was a 0:34 one-verse clip; the 2:09 one was actually "Eensey Weensey Spider", a different variant).
