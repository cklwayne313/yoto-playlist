## How to use it

Naming a feeling is the first step to managing it, and a two-year-old needs the
face, the word and the action together. The last three tracks slow the card down
on purpose: breathe, name sad, name angry.

## Notes

- **Overlap:** `If You're Happy` also appears on `happy-songs`. It's repeated because it's the clearest happy/action frame in the library.
- `Breathe In & Out` is the one to keep for a real meltdown — sing it slowly, don't play it at volume.
- The last track is a check-in, not a song to sing along to: ask the question and wait for a point or a word.
