**Tracks:** 9 · **Runtime:** 18:29 · **Status:** verified

## What it is

Nine songs you do rather than listen to: clap, jump, turn, left hand, right hand.
For a late talker these are the useful ones — he can copy the movement before he
can say the word, and the movement is what makes the word stick.

No jingle, so only the lead-in: **0:00–0:02**. `—` means start at 0:00.
