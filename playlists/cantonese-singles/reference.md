## What it is

The loose songs: greetings (早晨歌, 午安歌, 拜拜歌), the queue, the bear hunting
honey, the postman, a kite-cloud, the festivals, and two long ones at the end.
With this card the five albums are complete — together they cover the 100-song
compilation.

## Trimming the intro

Same rule as the other 志安哥哥 cards: most videos open with the **~10.5 s**
jingle (聽歌學講廣東話) then a gap, so the cut point is measured per track.

- **Trim** is where the song starts — cut everything before it.
- `—` means nothing worth cutting.
- `0:10`–`0:15` is the full jingle.
