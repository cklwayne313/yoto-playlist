# 跳跳歌 — Sunshine Cantonese rhymes

**Inspired by:** the 30-track Cantonese playlist sold as a ready-made Make Your
Own card (`天晴童謠・粵語兒歌30首`), split into two cards so each one is a session
rather than a shelf. Every track is the **official upload** from [天晴童謠
Sunshine Nursery Rhyme](https://www.youtube.com/@sunshinenurseryrhyme).
**Cover:** `cover.jpg` — a full-bleed painted scene, titled **跳跳歌**. No character: these are collection cards, and a borrowed mascot on a Cantonese song card reads as a sticker. Background and lettering are ours. Not a Yoto card.
**Tracks:** 15 · **Runtime:** 37:47 · **Status:** verified

## Why two cards, not one

The seller's card holds 30 songs and 1h18m. That is a catalogue: a two-year-old
cannot choose from it, and a card that long never repeats the same five songs,
which is the part that builds words. Splitting it by **energy** gives two cards
that each do one job — one to jump and sing to, one to wind down to.

Two differences from the seller's list, both honest:

- their **貼心歌** (1:53) is the channel's **點心歌** (2:04) — dim sum song; no
  upload is called 貼心歌.
- their **何家公雞何家猜** is the channel's **何家小雞何家猜** (2:45).
- 五隻鴨仔 is not here: it already lives on `cantonese-nursery-rhymes`.

## How to use it

Play the first five on repeat; they are short enough that five in a row is
still under ten minutes. Sing the line, then stop and look at him — 有隻八腳蜘蛛
and 老伯塊田係牧場 both have a pause built in for exactly that.
