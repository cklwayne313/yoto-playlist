# Super Simple — Silly Songs

**Inspired by:** Super Simple's own card in the
[Super Simple Songs Collection](https://us.yotoplay.com/products/super-simple-songs-collection),
track list from [Super Simple](https://supersimple.com/article/yoto-and-super-simple-together-at-last)
**Cover:** `cover.jpg` — the card's own art, from the Yoto collection page
**Tracks:** 15 · **Runtime:** 40:31 · **Status:** verified

The opposite of Bedtime. Nonsense words, wrong words, silly swaps and songs built to
make a two-year-old laugh. Humour is a language skill: jokes need prediction, and
prediction is what fills-in-the-blank singing trains.

## Notes

- Yoto lists the card as 40:25. Two tracks — `Apples & Bananas` and `Peanut Butter & Jelly` — aren't given a length on the track list, so the table uses the upload lengths; that's the 6-second difference.
- `Down By the Bay` is the loosest match: the card is 2:26, the video 2:48 (the video repeats a verse). Same recording family, just longer.
- `I Can't Remember the Words to This Song` is the most useful track here for speech: it deliberately gets the words wrong, so he has to notice and correct.
- `10 Apples on My Head` is long (5:24) — that's the card, not a compilation.
