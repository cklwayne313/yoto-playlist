## How to use it

Mealtime language: naming food, saying what you like, and the two-word exchange
that matters most — `more`, `no`, `yum`, `yuck`. Several of these are
choice questions, so sing the question and let him answer with a word, a sign or
a point.

- **New to the library:** both `Do You Like…` songs, Are You Hungry, Apples & Bananas, Fruit Salad, Ice Cream, Pizza Party, Pat-A-Cake, Healthy Eating.
