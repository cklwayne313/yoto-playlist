# Super Simple — Bedtime Songs

**Inspired by:** Super Simple's own card in the
[Super Simple Songs Collection](https://us.yotoplay.com/products/super-simple-songs-collection),
track list from [Super Simple](https://supersimple.com/article/yoto-and-super-simple-together-at-last)
**Cover:** `cover.jpg` — the card's own art, from the Yoto collection page
**Tracks:** 15 · **Runtime:** 37:51 · **Status:** verified

The lullaby card of the set. Slow, quiet, one voice or a small band — no drums,
no dance breaks. This is the one to put on after the last story, when you want him
to stop moving rather than start.

## Notes

- All fifteen are official Super Simple uploads; where a Topic (audio-only) version exists it's used, because it matches the card without the video intro.
- `Beddy-Bye Butterfly` is the card's opener and its best track — a genuine lullaby with a story, not a nursery rhyme sung slowly.
- Durations are the card's. The linked videos run a few seconds longer where they include an intro; the biggest gaps are none over 6 seconds here.
- If he's still awake after `My Teddy Bear`, play the card again rather than switching cards — the sameness is the signal.
