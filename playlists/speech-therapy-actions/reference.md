## How to use it

Ten Noodle & Pals action songs in a row. The point is the verb, not the tune:
stand, sit, walk, stomp, jump, turn, stop. Do every action with him and say the
verb as you do it — one word, then the phrase.

## Notes

- **Every track is new to the library** — nothing here repeats another card.
- `Noodle Says` is this card's engine: it's Simon Says, so it trains listening and inhibition as well as verbs.
- Keep it to five or six tracks per session. Ten in a row is a workout, not a lesson.
