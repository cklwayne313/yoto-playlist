## Why this card first

Of everything in the 100-song compilation, this album is the one aimed at his
age: eating, teeth, bath, toilet, bottle, blanket, feet. Those are the words a
late talker needs first, because they happen at the same time every day and you
can act them out while the song plays.

The full 100-song card runs 4h11m. This is 25 minutes, one album, and it will
get played.

## Trimming the intro

Most 志安哥哥 videos open with the same **~10.5 s** branded jingle (聽歌學講廣東話)
followed by a gap. The gap varies, so the trim point is per track, measured, not
assumed:

- **Trim column** below is where the song itself starts. Cut everything before it.
- `0:01` means there is almost nothing to cut — just the lead-in.
- 齊齊拍 is the one here with the full jingle: **cut 0:12**.
- Long lead-ins worth cutting: 吹泡泡 0:05, 小腳板 0:04, 口罩超人 0:03.

Measured by matching the jingle's envelope against a reference copy, then finding
the first silence gap after it — not by ear and not by guess.
