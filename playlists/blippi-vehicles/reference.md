# Blippi Vehicle Songs

**Inspired by:** https://us.yotoplay.com/products/blippi-vehicle-songs
**Cover:** `cover.jpg` — the card's own art, from the Yoto product page
**Tracks:** 12 · **Runtime:** 32:30 · **Status:** verified

Twelve vehicle songs, one per truck. Each one names the parts and the job —
excavator digs, garbage truck lifts, fire truck sprays — so the vocabulary is concrete and
the song structure repeats. Good age-2 fit: short, loud, and about things he can point at.
