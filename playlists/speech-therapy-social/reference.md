## How to use it

Social language before social skills: greet, name, ask, share, thank, say goodbye.
These are short, scripted exchanges — exactly what a two-year-old can learn as a
whole phrase before he can build one.

## Notes

- **New to the library:** everything except `Skidamarink`, which also appears on `best-of-the-wiggles` in a different recording.
- `What's Your Name?` is the one to use with a mirror or a photo album — names are the strongest motivator at this age.
- Model the whole phrase ("my turn"), not the single word, then pause for any part of it.
